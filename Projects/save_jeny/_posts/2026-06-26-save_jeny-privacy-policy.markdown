<h1>Privacy Policy for Save Jeny</h1>

<h2>Introduction</h2>
<p>Welcome to <strong>Save Jeny!</strong> We are committed to protecting your privacy. Because Save Jeny is a fully offline game, your data remains entirely in your hands. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled.</p>

<h2>No Online Accounts Required</h2>
<p>Save Jeny does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to play. You can enjoy the full game completely offline.</p>

<h2>Local Data Storage and Usage</h2>
<p>The game saves your progress, high scores, and settings <strong>solely on your local device</strong>. No online cloud saves are used, and no data is transmitted over the internet.</p>

<h2>Data Security and Responsibility</h2>
<p>Because your save files are stored locally on your device, they are as secure as your phone or tablet itself. Please note that we do not have access to your device; if your device is lost, stolen, damaged, or cleared, we cannot recover your game progress for you.</p>

<h2>Third-Party Access and Developer Access</h2>
<p>We <strong>do not</strong> collect, track, sell, share, or distribute any of your data. As developers, we have zero access to your device's save files or gameplay history. Your data belongs entirely to you.</p>

<h2>Third-Party Platform Collection</h2>
<p>While the game itself functions entirely offline, please be aware that the store platforms you use to download or purchase the game (such as Google Play or itch.io) may automatically collect certain device data, distribution statistics, or payment information in accordance with their own respective privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for the data practices of these individual storefronts.</p>

<h2>Enjoy Save Jeny</h2>
<p>We hope you enjoy playing <strong>Save Jeny</strong> with peace of mind, knowing that your gaming experience is 100% private and secure.</p>

<p>If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to <a class="linkhai" href="/contactus">Contact Us</a>.</p>
